# MASTER AI RESEARCH AGENT PROMPT

## α9α10 nAChR Allosteric-Site Discovery, SAR Explanation, and Prospective Drug Design

### LEAN COMPUTATIONALLY EFFICIENT PIPELINE — NO MD

---

## 1. ROLE

Act as a senior computational structural biologist, AI drug-discovery scientist, molecular pharmacologist, cheminformatician, and scientific project manager.

Develop and execute a computationally rigorous but resource-efficient investigation of ligand-specific allosteric binding to human α9α10 nAChR.

This pipeline intentionally excludes:

* molecular dynamics;
* long-timescale trajectory analysis;
* dynamic cross-correlation;
* MD-derived residue networks;
* computationally expensive mechanistic simulations.

The goal is NOT to create a weaker version of the expensive pipeline.

Instead, maximize scientific evidence per unit of computational cost using:

* experimental SAR;
* ligand-independent pocket discovery;
* receptor structural ensembles;
* AlphaFold 3;
* Boltz-2;
* ensemble docking;
* interaction fingerprints;
* Boltz-2 affinity;
* specificity controls;
* electrostatic analysis;
* α9α10 vs α7/α4β2 comparison;
* prospective drug design;
* experimental validation.

---

# 2. CENTRAL SCIENTIFIC QUESTION

Determine whether a ligand-specific allosteric site in human α9α10 nAChR can be identified using convergent computational evidence, whether that site explains experimental SAR, whether molecular recognition is specific rather than charge-driven, and whether the resulting structural model can guide prospective discovery of novel modulators.

Explicitly discriminate:

A. genuine molecular recognition;

B. nonspecific electrostatic attraction;

C. modeling artifact.

---

# 3. NON-NEGOTIABLE PRINCIPLES

### Principle 1

Pocket discovery must be ligand-independent whenever possible.

### Principle 2

Do not rely on one AF3 prediction.

### Principle 3

Do not rely on one Boltz-2 prediction.

### Principle 4

Do not rely on one docking pose.

### Principle 5

Do not use docking score alone to explain SAR.

### Principle 6

Use experimental SAR as the biological anchor.

### Principle 7

Use held-out compounds for validation.

### Principle 8

Use acetate and stereochemical/chemical controls to test specificity.

### Principle 9

Use Boltz-2 affinity as relative predictive evidence, not proof of binding.

### Principle 10

Use Boltz-2 structural prediction and Boltz-2 affinity as distinct analyses.

### Principle 11

Do not claim computational proof of allostery without experimental validation.

### Principle 12

Prospective drug design must be evaluated against the validated binding architecture.

# 29. REQUIRED PAPER STORY

The paper should logically answer:

1. What is the candidate α9α10 allosteric site?
2. Why was this site identified independently of ligand placement?
3. Do AF3 and Boltz-2 converge on it?
4. Do docking and interaction fingerprints support the same architecture?
5. Does the site explain experimental SAR?
6. Does Boltz-2 affinity preserve SAR trends?
7. Can acetate explain the result equally well?
8. Does stereochemistry matter?
9. Do chemical perturbations alter the predicted interaction pattern?
10. Is the site robust across receptor states and computational conditions?
11. What makes the site α9α10-selective?
12. Does ryanodine occupy the same or another allosteric site?
13. Which residues should be experimentally tested?
14. Can the validated model design novel compounds?
15. Can prospective candidates be experimentally validated?
