SECTION 4. CHEN ET AL. QC LAYER (external benchmark applied as-is)
================================================================================
The reference paper's concrete allosteric-modulator findings, applied verbatim:
  a) "Allosteric modulators remain one of the most challenging categories for
     AF3 and Boltz-2." Their allosteric set (WRN, EGFR, GAC, KRAS SII, SHP2):
     both models *generally failed to reproduce the correct allosteric binding
     modes*. Failure mode = induced-pocket geometry / local rearrangement
     recovery, not ligand placement per se.
  b) Single success was a *preorganized, compact, fragment* pocket (SHP2
     fragment), i.e., low conformational ambiguity — the exception.
  c) minPAE < 0.85 Å fraction: 28.6% for their allosteric ligands vs 73.7%
     for canonical orthosteric ligands. Low minPAE is their pose-accuracy
     discriminator.
  d) Directed guidance: "...predicted allosteric poses should therefore be
     treated primarily as hypotheses and interpreted alongside experimental
     structures, SAR, mutagenesis, biophysical mapping, or physics-based
     simulations before being used to guide analog design."
  e) Boltz-2 affinity captured relative trends in SELECTED series only; not
     quantitative; varies across series.
Applied to Site 23 / this dataset:
  - Every AF3 ligand_minPAE in Table4 lies in [12.16, 25.24] Å over all
    175 tabulated rows. minPAE < 0.85 Å fraction = 0.0 %. Even by the loose
    "minPAE < 10 Å" bar, 0 rows qualify. By Chen et al.'s own filter, this
    dataset contains NO high-confidence ligand pose anywhere, including
    Site 23. (This is consistent with, not contrary to, the paper.)
  - Site 23 is a vestibular ECD inter-subunit cavity of the pentamer with
    multiple pose clusters (Section 2): it displays the "conformational
    ambiguity" regime, NOT the preorganized SHP2-fragment regime.
  - Therefore: Chen et al. license us to call Site 23 an *allosteric binding
    HYPOTHESIS* whose downstream use requires SAR/mutagenesis/biophysics.
    It does NOT license pose-level confidence. Any analog-design decision may
    proceed only as a test of the hypothesis, with SAR + mutagenesis as the
    arbiter, per (d).
Verdict 4: The external benchmark DEMOTES all pose-level claims and DEMOTES
"HIGH_CONFIDENCE" to "HYPOTHESIS support". This is the single most important
import of the paper into this project.

SECTION 8. EXPERIMENTAL SAR VALIDATION
================================================================================
Dataset: 30 compounds, 7 active, 23 inactive (modulator-dataset-a9a10.csv).
SAR scoring in Table10: Site 23 sar_score = 0.5066 (labelled STRONG); this is
the ONLY STRONG-SAR label in the table. However:
  a) Docking-vs-IC50 at Site 23 (VALIDATION_REPORT_SAR.md): Pearson r = 0.44,
     R2 = 0.19, Spearman rho = 0.32, n = 7 actives — WEAK, insignificant
     (nothing controls for the 6-order-of-magnitude potency spread).
  b) Mechanistic-SAR (30 compounds): binding-only AUROC 0.51 (near random);
     chemical-only 0.55; full model 0.64 but NOT significant (paired bootstrap
     p = 0.523). No single feature significant (permutation p > 0.27 for every
     feature; docking p=0.42, boltz2_affinity p=0.27, confidence p=0.74).
  c) F2 (stereochemistry): L-ASC vs D-ASC site-occupancy overlap = 0.931,
     chi2 p = 0.601 → FAIL. The enantiomers are NOT distinguished. This is the
     strongest falsification result in the whole battery and directly
     contradicts the Sep-4 report's claim that "L-form shows 2-3x higher
     enrichment" (that older claim is a false positive; retract).
  d) F1 (electrostatics): acetate vs L-ASC overlap only 0.379, p = 1.86e-14 →
     PASS; charge-only explanation is NOT sufficient. This is a genuine
     specific-recognition hint (the anionic charge alone cannot explain the
     placements).
  e) F3 (protonation): anion vs neutral overlap 0.827 → PASS; placement not a
     protonation artifact.
  f) F8 (size/posing artifact): L-ASC vs ryanodine overlap 0.235 (PASS);
     the pocket-locality is not generic-size-driven.
Consolidated: the SAR layer supports (i) a cavity that specifically refuses
to be explained by charge alone (F1), and (ii) a cavity pattern reproducible
across states (F3/F4), but (iii) it does NOT support potency ranking (a/c),
activity prediction (b), or enantiomer recognition (c).
Verdict 8: SAR VALIDATION IS MIXED-TO-NEGATIVE. One strong PASS (F1) and one
hard FAIL (F2). Potency/SAR-quantitative claims are NOT supported.
