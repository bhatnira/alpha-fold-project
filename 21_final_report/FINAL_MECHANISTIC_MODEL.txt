Title: De Novo Discovery of a Positive Allosteric Modulator Binding Site on the α9α10 Nicotinic Acetylcholine Receptor

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Using an integrated computational pipeline of AlphaFold 3 co-folding (400 models), Boltz-2 co-folding (90 models), AutoDock Vina docking (480/480 successful), and systematic falsification testing (7/8 passed), we identified Site 23 as the leading candidate PAM binding site on the α9α10 nAChR. Site 23 is located at the α9(+)/α10(-) ECD inter-subunit interface, classified as a vestibular inter-subunit pocket. It is constitutive (present in both stoichiometries), passes 7/8 falsification tests, and shows cross-method convergence between AF3 and Boltz-2. However, no site achieved HIGH_CONFIDENCE classification, and stereochemical discrimination (F2) was not achieved. Docking completed for all 30 compounds × 8 sites × 2 stoichiometries. The model is best described as a "computationally supported and SAR-consistent hypothesis" requiring experimental validation. KEY DOCKING FINDING: Site 23 shows weak binding with no active/inactive discrimination; TMD sites (34, 35) show stronger binding overall.
